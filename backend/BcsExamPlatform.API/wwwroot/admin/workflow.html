📚 Exam Management Workflow

Step-by-step guide to creating and publishing exams

1

Create New Exam

Where: Dashboard or Exams page

Button: + Create New Exam

What to fill:

  • Exam name (English & Bangla)
  • Start date and time
  • Duration (e.g., 120 minutes)
  • Total questions (e.g., 200)
  • Total marks (e.g., 200)
  • Subject distribution (Bangla: 50, English: 50, Math: 100)

Result: Exam created with Draft status

↓
2

Generate Questions

Where: Exams page, find your exam

Button: Generate Questions

What happens:

  • System automatically selects questions from question bank
  • Questions match your subject distribution
  • Difficulty levels are respected (Easy, Intermediate, Hard)
  • Total questions match your exam configuration
💡 Tip: Make sure you have enough questions in your question bank. Use "Quick Setup" → "Seed 200 Sample Questions" if needed.
↓
3

Publish Exam

Where: Exams page, same exam row

Button: Publish

What happens:

  • Exam status changes to Published
  • Exam becomes visible to students
  • Students can register and take the exam
⚠️ Important: Once published, students can start taking the exam. Make sure everything is correct before publishing!
↓
4

Monitor & Manage

Available actions for published exams:

  • View - See exam details, questions, and student attempts
  • Unpublish - Take exam offline (if needed)

View exam details to see:

  • Overview: All exam settings and configuration
  • Questions: Full list of questions in the exam
  • Student Attempts: Who took the exam and their scores
  • Analytics: Performance statistics

📊 Status Reference

Status Meaning Available Actions
Draft Exam created but not ready for students Generate Questions, Publish
Published Exam is live and available to students View, Unpublish
