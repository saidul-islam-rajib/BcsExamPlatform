🚀 Quick Start: Your First Exam

Follow these 3 simple steps

⚠️ Important: Questions First!

Before creating exams, you need questions in your question bank. Without questions, you can't generate exam questions!

1 Add Questions to Question Bank

You need questions before you can create exams. Choose one option:

Option A: Quick Setup (Recommended for testing)
  1. Go to Dashboard
  2. Click "Quick Setup" tab
  3. Click Seed 200 Sample Questions button
  4. Wait for confirmation (creates 50 Bangla + 50 English + 100 Math questions)
Option B: Manual Entry
  1. Use the API to add your own questions
  2. Or create a question entry form (future feature)

2 Create Your Exam

  1. Click + Create New Exam button
  2. Fill in exam details:
    • Name: "My First Exam"
    • Date & Time: Choose when it starts
    • Duration: 10 minutes (for testing)
    • Total Questions: 10
    • Total Marks: 10
  3. Add Subject Distribution:
    • Click + Add Subject
    • Select "বাংলা" (Bangla)
    • Total Questions: 10
    • Easy: 5, Intermediate: 3, Hard: 2
  4. Click Create Exam
Result: Exam created with "Draft" status (no questions yet!)

3 Generate Questions & Publish

  1. Go to Exams page
  2. Find your exam (status: Draft)
  3. Click Generate Questions button
    • System automatically selects 10 Bangla questions
    • Respects difficulty distribution (5 easy, 3 intermediate, 2 hard)
  4. Click Publish button
  5. Click View → Questions tab to see all 10 questions!

✅ That's It!

Your exam is now live with 10 questions. Students can take it!

❓ Troubleshooting

Q: I don't see any questions in my exam?

A: You need to click "Generate Questions" button first. The exam is created empty, questions are added separately.

Q: "Generate Questions" fails with error?

A: You don't have enough questions in your question bank. Go to Dashboard → Quick Setup → Seed Sample Questions.

Q: Can I change questions after generating?

A: Yes! Click "Generate Questions" again to get a new random selection from your question bank.
